Getting started

BackAgain?! puts a moment of friction between you and the apps you open without deciding to. It does not block them forever and it does not judge you: it asks why you are there, gives you exactly the time you asked for, and keeps honest receipts.

Setting it up

  1. Allow Screen Time access. BackAgain?! uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks to place a shield over the apps you choose. iOS asks for your permission once, with your passcode or Face ID.
  2. Pick your usual suspect. Choose the app you open most automatically. The free plan protects up to two apps; start with one.
  3. That's it. The next time you open that app, the shield appears first.
BackAgain?! never sees what you do inside an app, and your history never leaves your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

The shield

When you open a protected app, the shield comes first. You have two choices:

  • Never mind closes the app. It counts as walked away: the best thing you can do, and it lifts your score.
  • Choose reason asks what you came for, and opens the app for that long only.

The three reasons

ReasonForTime (at Calm)
In and outA reply, a quick check of one thing2 min
One thing. Really.A specific task you can name5 min
Scroll, honestlyBrowsing, and admitting it10 min

When the time is up, the shield comes back on its own. The times get shorter as the adaptive level rises (see below).

Four different shields

  • An ordinary open: the usual question.
  • A quick return: you came back within minutes of closing it, and the shield says so.
  • Straight from another protected app: hopping from one to the next is noticed too.
  • The end of a session: the time you asked for is over. Now and then it asks "Was that worth it?"; your answers make the protection stricter or gentler.

Adaptive protection

Each protected app has a level, shown in the Shield tab. It rises when your use starts to look like a loop and eases off when it calms down. Nothing is hidden: tap Why these levels in Shield to see exactly what moved it.

LevelWhat changes
CalmNormal friction and the full times.
BusySlightly shorter sessions.
HeavyShorter again: 2, 3 and 5 minutes.
Looping"Scroll, honestly" is no longer offered.
RelentlessNo reasons at all. The only way in is an emergency pass, if you have one left.

What raises it

  • Coming straight back after a session ends.
  • Several opens in a short window.
  • Overrides and emergency passes.
  • Opening at the time of day when you usually slip.
  • Answering "Not worth it" at the end of sessions.
  • Jumping straight from one protected app to another.

What lowers it

  • Time: quiet periods bring it back down on their own.
  • Planning a session in advance.
  • Ending a session before the timer does.

Sessions, planned sessions and End now

A running session

While a session runs, a countdown appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Tap End now there to stop early: the shield comes straight back over the app, without opening BackAgain?!, and ending early counts in your favour.

Planned sessions

From the app's card in Shield, Start a session… lets you decide the reason and the length before you open the app. After a short wait of 45 seconds (the wait is what shows you meant it), you get up to one and a half times what the shield would give, and a lower level afterwards.

  • Planned sessions a day depend on your commitment level: 4, 3 or 2.
  • They are not available at Relentless.
  • One session per app at a time.

The daily limit

Every protected app has a daily limit: the most time it gets in a day, however you get in. It starts at 90 minutes and can be set from 15 minutes to 3 hours, in 15-minute steps, from the app's card in Shield.

  • Lowering it is instant.
  • Raising it costs a one-minute wait, plus your PIN if you set one. Your very first change is free.
  • When it's reached, the app stays shielded until midnight, whatever its level.
  • Lifting a limit you already reached lets you back in, and costs your score the most of anything.

Time is counted by iOS in steps of a quarter of the limit, so BackAgain?! shows how much you have used as "at least".

Emergency passes

For when it really is urgent. A pass opens a protected app for 10 minutes with no questions, even at Relentless, but not past a daily limit you have reached. Passes come back on a rolling seven days.

Commitment levelPasses per 7 days
Easy out5
Make me wait3
Don't let me wiggle out1

A pass is an honest way around the shield, and it still counts against your score.

In Settings, How hard should we make it? sets how much effort it takes to weaken your own protection.

  • PIN. An optional four-digit PIN guards the settings that weaken protection, such as raising a limit. It never guards the shield itself. It is stored on your iPhone as a salted hash.
  • Pause the rules (in Shield) switches protection off until you turn it back on. Turning it back on is always instant.
  • Stop protecting an app (on its card) always waits at least a minute first.

Today and the Under control score

Today shows your Under control score, from 0 to 100, how it compares with yesterday, and four numbers: how many times you let yourself in, your back-agains, how often you walked away, and how much of your daily limit you have used. Below, one line says what the day was mostly about, with a next step when there is one.

Tap the score to see exactly what moved it. Every day starts at 100:

What happenedEffect
Each time you let yourself in, after the first two of the day−3
Each back-again (in again within 20 minutes of a session ending)−5
Each open inside a burst (three or more in 15 minutes)−3
Each override or emergency pass−5
Your daily limit, on a curve: a quarter used, half, all of it−3 · −10 · −40
Each time you lift a limit you already reached−15
Each time you walk away (up to +10)+2
Each session you end early (up to +10)+2

There is no ceiling on the costs: doing the wrong thing again always costs again. The score itself never goes below 0 or above 100.

The score is a receipt of what you did, not a judgement of who you are, and not a medical or psychological measure.

Patterns

  • One thing to notice. The strongest pattern in your last 28 days (a time of day, a day of the week, one app carrying the loop), with the evidence and a next step. PRO shows every pattern found.
  • Charts. Your score over time, your back-agains per day, and your minutes against your daily limit. Only days with recorded data are shown.
  • The week in numbers, what you said you needed, and which app got you.
  • Ranges. The last 7 days on Free; 1, 3, 6 months and a year with PRO. Your history stays on your iPhone for up to a year.

Widgets, Live Activity and Focus

  • Widgets. Add BackAgain?! to your Home Screen, small or medium, for your score and today's numbers at a glance. During a session, both show the countdown with a stop button.
  • Live Activity. Each planned session shows a countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with End now.
  • Focus PRO. In iPhone Settings › Focus, add the BackAgain?! filter to a Focus: Work keeps friction slightly raised, Sleep keeps it raised. It can only make protection stricter, never weaker.
  • Shortcuts and Siri PRO. Raise friction for a while, restore it, or ask for today's score, from Shortcuts, automations or Siri.
  • Weekly receipt PRO. One notification a week with what changed. No streaks, no "we miss you".

Plans

Yearly, with a free week the first time; monthly; or once, for good. Current prices are shown in the app before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them.

Restore, manage, cancel

  • Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you used.
  • Manage or cancel: Settings › Manage subscription in BackAgain?!, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When Pro ends

BackAgain?! keeps protecting your first two apps. Any others are paused, not protected, but kept with their history, and protected again if Pro comes back.

Troubleshooting

"The shield is off" when I open BackAgain?!

Screen Time access was switched off. Tap the button on that screen to allow it again, or check iPhone Settings › Screen Time.

A protected app opened without the shield

Open BackAgain?! once: it checks every protected app and puts back any shield that went missing. A session may also still be running: its countdown is on the Lock Screen.

An app is blocked and it shouldn't be

Check whether its daily limit is reached (its card in Shield says so) or whether it is at Relentless. If neither, open BackAgain?! and then write to us.

The countdown isn't on the Lock Screen

Make sure Live Activities are on for BackAgain?! in iPhone Settings › BackAgain?!. Opening the app restores a missing countdown for a planned session still running.

The widget is out of date

iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule. Opening BackAgain?! updates it straight away.

Removing BackAgain?!

Deleting the app removes its shields and deletes its history from your iPhone.

Your data

No account. No ads. No analytics. Your protected apps, sessions and history stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us. Purchases are handled by Apple. The details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms and Conditions.

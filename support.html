Support

Something not working? Most answers are below. If not, write to us.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Write to us: apps.supporthub@gmail.com — tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

A protected app won't open, or the shield is stuck

Open BackAgain?! once: it checks and repairs the shield every time it comes to the front. If an app is still blocked when it shouldn't be, check Settings › Screen Time on your iPhone, then write to us.

Stop protecting an app

In BackAgain?!, open Shield, tap the app, then "Stop protecting this app". There is a short wait first — on purpose.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in BackAgain?! and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › BackAgain?! Pro. Or in BackAgain?!: Settings › Manage subscription. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your history stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us. See the Privacy Policy.

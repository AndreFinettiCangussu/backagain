The licence

Your use of BackAgain?! is governed by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (the "Standard EULA"). These terms add to it; where they differ, the Standard EULA prevails.

Free and Pro

The free version protects up to two apps and includes the shield, the adaptive protection, the daily limit and your score. BackAgain?! Pro adds features such as more protected apps, longer history and additional insights, as described in the app.

Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may change for new purchases.

Subscriptions, trials and cancellation

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period.
  • You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period stays active until it ends.
  • A free trial, where offered, is available once per Apple ID. Any unused part of a trial ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

When Pro ends

If Pro ends, BackAgain?! keeps working as the free version: it keeps protecting your first two apps. Any other protected apps are paused, no longer protected, but kept with their history and protected again if you return to Pro.

What BackAgain?! is not

BackAgain?! is a self-help tool for more deliberate phone use. It is not a medical, psychological or therapeutic service, and its score and insights are not a diagnosis. It relies on Apple's Screen Time frameworks, which you can switch off at any time; the developer does not guarantee that any app will always be blocked.

Your privacy

Privacy Policy

BackAgain helps you manage access to apps you choose to protect. Your behavioral history stays on your device; the developer does not receive it.

Last updated: September 24, 2026

In short: BackAgain does not require an account, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or send your protected-app choices, sessions or behavioral history to a developer-operated server.

Who provides BackAgain

BackAgain is developed by Andrè Finetti Cangussù. For privacy questions or data requests, contact apps.supporthub@gmail.com.

Data used on your device

To provide the features you choose to use, BackAgain processes and stores locally:

  • The apps you choose to protect, represented by Apple's Screen Time tokens. When available, an app name may be stored locally so accessibility features can identify it.
  • Preferences and settings, such as daily limits, protection level, reminders and an optional PIN. The PIN is stored as a salted hash, not in plain text. Screen Time permission status is checked locally.
  • Session and protection events, including times, granted duration, selected intent, returns to an app, Emergency Pass use and any answers to prompts. These events support shields, statistics and troubleshooting.

BackAgain does not read your messages, what you post or the content you view inside protected apps.

Storage and sharing

App data is kept in local iOS storage, including an App Group shared by BackAgain, its Screen Time extensions and its widget. BackAgain does not transmit that data to the developer or to advertising or analytics services. Device backups, if any, are managed by iOS under your Apple settings.

TestFlight and support

If you use a TestFlight build, Apple may make beta metrics, crash reports and the feedback you submit through TestFlight available to the developer, including screenshots you attach. These channels do not automatically upload the behavioral history stored by BackAgain.

If you email us, we receive your sender address and message. We use them to respond and handle your request, keeping them only as long as necessary or as required by applicable obligations.

Retention and your choices

When a new event is recorded, the local event log removes entries older than 365 days and applies a technical limit of 100,000 events. You can clear it in Settings > Diagnostics > Clear log. You can change or remove protected apps, revoke Screen Time or notification permissions in iOS Settings, and uninstall the app. Any backup copies are governed by your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. For questions, contact apps.supporthub@gmail.com.
